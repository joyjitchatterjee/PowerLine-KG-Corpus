The 255 JPEG crops and original component/condition metadata derive from **InsPLAD: A Dataset and Benchmark for Power Line Asset Inspection in UAV Images**.

Credit: Andre Luiz Buarque Vieira e Silva, Heitor de Castro Felix, Francisco Paulo Magalhaes Simoes, Veronica Teichrieb, Michel M. dos Santos, Hemir Santiago, Virginia Sgotti, and Henrique B. Lott Neto. See the publication for accented names and full bibliographic details.

Original paths and image hashes are retained in the annotation records. Human descriptions, recommendations, notes and template-generated companion descriptions are additions from this project, not original InsPLAD text annotations. Their source labels are retained separately.
